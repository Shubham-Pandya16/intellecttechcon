RFID for Library

RFID technology uses radio waves to identify and track library materials, enabling seamless borrowing experiences, automated check-in/out, and enhanced security. Step into the future of library services with RFID and enjoy a new level of convenience in operations.

The Future of Library Operations


RFID tags are embedded in library materials such as books, CDs and DVDs, and can be read and programmed by RFID readers throughout the library. Each tag contains a unique identifier — effectively a digital fingerprint — used to track the material's location, circulation status, and other pertinent details. Library patrons can simply wave their books at self-service kiosks, instantly updating their accounts and saving time while ensuring accurate inventory management.

Benefits


  • Time Saving for Both Librarian and Patron: RFID reduces manual book check-in/out times, allowing librarians to handle multiple transactions efficiently and enhancing overall service.
  • Cost Saving in the Long Run: Despite initial implementation costs, RFID's automation and inventory loss prevention contribute to significant long-term savings.
  • Improved Circulation Process: Automates routine tasks with quicker simultaneous scanning of multiple items and instant circulation status updates.
  • Efficient Stock Management: Real-time tracking enables effective stock management and easy identification of misplaced items.
  • Excellent Security: RFID enhances security by integrating with Turnstile systems, reducing theft and maintaining collection integrity.
  • Data Analytics of Patrons: Provides insights into patron preferences and book status to aid collection development decisions. Compatible with SIP2/NCIP integration.
